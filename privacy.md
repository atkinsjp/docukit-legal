# DocuKit Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 9, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Atkins Media, LLC (“Atkins Media,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use DocuKit: PDF Editor & Scanner (“DocuKit”).

## 1. Information we process

### Account information

We process account information such as your email address, name when supplied through Sign in with Apple, authentication records, and an internal user identifier. Sign in with Apple may provide an Apple private-relay email address.

### Content you choose to submit

When you use an online feature, we may process the files and content you select, including PDFs, documents, images, videos, audio recordings, entered text, prompts, chat messages, extracted text, filenames, and generated results.

### Activity and service information

We may process account-scoped tool history, generated-file metadata, processing-job status, timestamps, request diagnostics, and security or operational logs. Infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, device or browser information, and similar technical data needed to deliver and protect the service.

## 2. Local processing

Many document, PDF, image-conversion, text, and encryption operations run on your device. Content that remains entirely on your device is not collected by us.

The Document Locker encrypts files locally using AES-256-GCM with a passphrase-derived key. DocuKit does not store or transmit the locker passphrase. Encryption does not delete the original file.

## 3. Online and AI processing

Uploading is an explicit action. When you use an online feature, DocuKit sends the selected content to our server and, when necessary, a service provider to complete your request.

Optional AI features may send selected text, prompts, documents, images, or audio to OpenAI or Google for document-related processing such as summarization, grammar review, translation, transcription, speech generation, document assistance, or background removal.

Before an AI request is submitted, DocuKit explains that the selected content will leave the device and asks you to continue or cancel. Do not submit content you are not authorized to process.

Provider processing is subject to the applicable provider terms and policies:

- [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/)
- [OpenAI business and API data privacy](https://openai.com/business-data/)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Gemini API Terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms)

Video compression and trimming upload the selected video to DocuKit’s server for processing. These tools do not generate video content.

## 4. How we use information

We use information to:

- Create, authenticate, secure, and support accounts.
- Perform the document or media action you request.
- Store and return account-scoped files, history, and job status.
- Detect abuse, enforce limits, troubleshoot failures, and protect the service.
- Comply with applicable law and enforce our Terms.

We do not use DocuKit data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

## 5. How information is shared

We share information only as needed with providers that support DocuKit, including authentication, hosting, infrastructure, file processing, and AI providers. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or as part of a business transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell personal information.

## 6. Storage and retention

Device-library files are stored locally. Removing the app may remove local copies, while files exported to Files or another app remain in those locations.

Server-processed files, generated results, history, and job information may be retained in your private account workspace for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain security and operational records, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations. Provider retention practices may also apply to content sent for processing.

Deleting a device copy does not delete a server copy or an exported copy.

## 7. Security

We use authentication, account-scoped access controls, encrypted network connections, input validation, usage limits, and other safeguards designed to protect information. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 8. Your choices and deletion

You can choose not to use online or AI features. You can cancel an AI disclosure without sending the selected content.

You can delete local files individually. To request deletion of your account and associated server data, use any deletion option provided in DocuKit or contact [support@atkinsmedia.io](mailto:support@atkinsmedia.io). We may retain limited information where required for legal, fraud-prevention, security, or dispute-resolution purposes.

## 9. Children

DocuKit is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

## 10. International processing

Our providers may process information in countries other than yours. Where required, we rely on contractual, legal, or other approved mechanisms for international transfers.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will post the updated version with a revised effective date.
